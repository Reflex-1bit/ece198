How to Play the Game

1. Choose a Difficulty

When the game starts, select a difficulty level using the buttons:

  • Button 1 → Easy (3-step pattern)
  • Button 2 → Medium (5-step pattern)
  • Button 3 → Hard (7-step pattern)

After selecting your difficulty, the system will ask you to click any button to begin.

2. Start the Round

After choosing the difficulty, press any button to begin showing the pattern.

3. Watch the LED Pattern

The three RGB LEDs will light up one at a time, showing a sequence made up of:

  • Which LED lights up (1, 2, or 3)
  • What color it shows (Red, Green, or Blue)

Watch carefully, as this is the pattern you must repeat.

4. Repeat the Pattern Using the Buttons

Each button controls one LED:

  • Button 1 → LED 1
  • Button 2 → LED 2
  • Button 3 → LED 3

Your number of clicks determines the color:

  • 1 click → RED
  • 2 clicks → GREEN
  • 3 clicks → BLUE

Example: If the pattern shows LED 3: Blue, press Button 3 three times (3 clicks). You must enter the sequence in the exact same order it was shown to get a point.

5. Completing a Round

Once you finish entering all steps, the game immediately checks your answer.

  • If you get the whole pattern right, your score increases.
  • If your total score reaches 3 or a multiple of 3, the motor activates: moving forward to raise a flag, then reversing as a reward.
  • If you get it wrong, the game prints the correct sequence on the Serial Monitor.

6. Continue or Quit

After each round:

  • Press Button 1 → Play again (you will choose difficulty again)
  • Press Button 2 or Button 3 → Quit the game

If you quit, the game shows your final score and the total number of rounds played.

7. Start Over Anytime

To play again after quitting, simply reset your Arduino board.
